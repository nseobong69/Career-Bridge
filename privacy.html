Career Bridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Career Bridge helps job seekers prepare job applications from their own experience. It is made up of this website and the Career Bridge browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. This page explains what information we collect, why, who else handles it and what you can do about it.

If you have a question or a request, email nseobongedem@gmail.com.

What we collect on the website

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our login provider (Supabase) and stored in a form we can't read.
  • Your profile: the details you choose to add, such as your phone number, address, skills, languages, registrations, driving licence and notice period. If you add your right-to-work situation (for example, whether you need visa sponsorship), we use it only to check whether you're eligible for a job.
  • Your CV: the file you upload, kept in private storage only your account can open, and the text we read from it.
  • Your Evidence Bank: the jobs you've held, the examples you write in your own words, and your qualifications.
  • Jobs and applications: the adverts you save, our checks of your fit, the application text we draft and you edit, your progress and the notes and status in your tracker.
  • Usage: a record of the AI credits you use, so we can apply your plan's monthly limit.
  • Payments: when paid plans are available, payments are handled by Paystack. We receive confirmation that you paid and which plan you chose, not your card details.

What the browser extension does

The extension only does something when you press a button in its popup.

  • When you log in, it sends your email and password to Career Bridge, then keeps a login token (not your password) in the extension's storage on your device until you log out.
  • It sends us the address of the page you open it on, so we can match the job and your application.
  • When you press "Save this advert", it sends us the advert's text.
  • To fill in a form, it reads the labels of the boxes on the page in your browser and fills them with the details you saved in Career Bridge. What you type into a job site isn't sent to us.
  • It never collects passwords for job sites, never answers questions about right to work, nationality, criminal records, health, equality monitoring or declarations, and never reads pages where you haven't pressed a button.

How we use your information

We use it to run Career Bridge for you: to check jobs against your evidence, to draft the parts of an application a form asks for, to fill in forms when you ask, to keep track of your applications, and to apply plan limits. We don't sell your information, we don't use it for advertising, and the website doesn't use tracking or analytics tools.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts our database, login system, file storage and server functions. Your account, profile, CV, Evidence Bank and applications are stored there.
  • Groq runs the AI that checks your fit and drafts text. When you use those features, it receives what it needs for that task: the advert text, your Evidence Bank and profile facts, your CV text, and any screenshots of application questions you upload.
  • Paystack processes payments, when paid plans are available.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it may keep technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs and open.er-api.com supply the website's fonts, code libraries and currency exchange rates. Your browser contacts them directly, so they see your IP address.

Some of these providers may process information outside the country you live in. Job listings come from public job feeds; we don't send your information to them.

Your browser

The website keeps your login in your browser's storage: until you log out if you tick "Remember me", otherwise until you close the tab. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping your information safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Our database is set up so each account can only see its own information, and CVs are kept in private storage. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use elsewhere.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can change or delete your profile, Evidence Bank entries and applications at any time on the website. If you ask us to close your account, we delete it and everything in it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email nseobongedem@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority, for example the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Career Bridge is for people applying for jobs. It isn't meant for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change makes a real difference to how we use your information, we'll also show a notice on the website.
