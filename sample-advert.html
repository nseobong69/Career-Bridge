Patient Experience Officer

Northfield Community Hospital · Band 4 (Agenda for Change) · £26,530 to £29,114 a year

Closing date: 31 December 2027

Job summary

You will be the first point of contact for patients and families who have a concern about their care, listening carefully and working with clinical teams to put things right quickly.

Main duties

  • Answer concerns from patients and families by phone, email and in person.
  • Keep an accurate record of every case and its outcome.
  • Draft formal responses for the complaints manager to sign.

Person specification

Essential criteria

  • Experience of handling complaints or concerns in a customer-facing role
  • Excellent written communication, including drafting formal responses
  • Experience of keeping accurate case records on a database

Desirable criteria

  • Experience of working in the public sector

Sponsorship

Applications from job seekers who require Skilled Worker sponsorship to work in the UK are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications.

How to apply

Complete the online application form. Shortlisting is based on the supporting information section, which is scored against the person specification, so give an example for each criterion.

Supporting information should be no more than 1500 words.

Trying Career Bridge on this page
